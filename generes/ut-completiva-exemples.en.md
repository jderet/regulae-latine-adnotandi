### UDante

> Unde caveant pugiles ne pretium constituant sibi causam; quia non tunc duellum, sed forum sanguinis et iustitie dicendum esset; nec tunc arbiter Deus adesse credatur, sed ille antiquus Hostis qui litigii fuerat persuasor.


*“Let champions therefore beware of making the prize their motive; for then it should be called not a duel but a market of blood and justice, nor should God be believed present as arbiter, but that ancient Enemy who had been the instigator of the quarrel.”*
