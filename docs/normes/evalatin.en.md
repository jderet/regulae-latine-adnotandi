# EvaLatin

EvaLatin is the evaluation campaign for Latin language-processing tools. It is organised by the CIRCSE and LiLa team (Milan) together with the LT4HALA workshop (*Language Technologies for Historical and Ancient Languages*).

| Edition | Tasks | Data |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 | lemmatisation, part of speech (UPOS) | Classical texts from [LASLA](lasla.md), with cross-genre and cross-time subtasks |
| 2022 | lemmatisation, part of speech, morphological features | LASLA texts, with new authors and periods |
| 2024 | dependency parsing, emotion polarity | texts annotated in UD following the [CIRCSE](circse.md) principles, in prose and verse |

## Why it is a standard

An evaluation campaign sets a target: systems are judged against a reference annotation, whose conventions bind anyone who wants to rank well. For lemmatisation and morphology the reference comes from LASLA; for syntax (2024), from CIRCSE. The `_poetry` and `_prose` sentence identifiers of [UD_Latin-CIRCSE](circse.md) mirror this 2024 division.
